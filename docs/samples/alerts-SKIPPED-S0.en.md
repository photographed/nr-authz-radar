## Anomaly detections (not notified by default)

Changes in volume or ratio produce many false positives, so they are not generated by default. `--include-anomaly` adds them to a separate policy with no notifications, for a daily review.

- D04 Surge in 403 / 404 ratio
- D10 Surge in authentication failures
